Crate Map

Drag a song onto a crate (or onto another song to place it before that one). Drag a crate's header onto another room. ✕ sends a song to the Removed tray, and you can drag it back. Each room shows its crate spots: a room past its spots needs a new spot built. A new room is a placeholder until Claude builds the space. Your edits stay in this browser until you copy them.
